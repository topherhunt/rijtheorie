- `vulnerables-giro-secuencia` (order of looks) and `vulnerables-giro-ciclista` (cyclist vanishing from the mirror) vs `afslaan-hombro` (what the shoulder look checks).
- `invoegen-file-lenta` vs `uitvoegen-fila-uit`: entering vs leaving a slow queue.
- `rotonde-quien-no-derecha` (especiales/06) vs `rotonde-47-bromfiets` (prioridad/12); `rotonde-carril-cuarto`, `rotonde-carril-dos` (prioridad/12) vs `especial-rotonde-carril-media` (different roundabout size or exit).

- `cambiar-carril-incorporacion` (posicion/04) and `bijzondere-manoeuvres-lista-situaciones` (prioridad/08): both multi lists of art. 54 situations; the lane-change list is kept as the lane-change lesson's own check.
